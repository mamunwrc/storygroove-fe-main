# 4. SimoneAI®: your Story Starter Kit

Simone is the first coach. She takes the idea in your head, walks you through twenty questions, and delivers a Story Starter Kit: a validated concept you can take into Olivia.

You can use Simone with a **$7 Story Starter Kit** purchase or with any **Builder** or **Studio** subscription. Builder and Studio include unlimited Simone. A $7 purchase buys **one new Simone conversation**.

## Before you start

On the dashboard, watch **Required First Step: Watch the Quick Start Tutorials**. The playlist covers Simone, Olivia, and Ellis. It is the fastest way to avoid starting the wrong kind of project.

If your subscription is paused or cancelled, the **Start a New Idea** button will not open a session. Resume or re-subscribe from **My Account → Subscription** first.

## Start a new idea

1. On the dashboard coach hub, click **Start a New Idea**.
2. In **Name of project**, enter a working title (for example `The Lost City`). You can rename it later.
3. Click **Talk to Simone**.

You land in Simone's chat. Click **Hey there writer! Click here to enter the SimoneAI® office.** That sends the conversation and Simone takes over.

Returning to an in-progress idea: open **My Work → Simone Ideas** and click **Chat with SimoneAI®**. You skip the "enter the office" step.

## The twenty questions

A **Story Starter Kit** progress strip stays at the top while intake is running: **Question 3 of 20** and how many are left.

Answer in your own words. You can:

- Type in **Ask me anything**
- Use voice dictation
- Attach files (up to 5 per message, 10 MB each: images, PDF, Word, text, markdown)

Simone is for **concept and market validation**, not manuscript pages. If a session drifts into full-draft feedback, it can pause with a message that points you to Ellis for chapter and manuscript work. The chat box then hides for that thread.

Around question 11 Simone asks for a working title. Your answer becomes the project name in the header (**Concept name**).

## When the kit arrives

Simone's last message is headed **Simone's Story Starter Kit for {your title}**. The progress strip disappears.

The kit typically includes a logline, synopsis, opening-scene concept, market readiness score, and a writing roadmap. Exact headings come from Simone's reply. Use **Copy** / **Copy for Word** on that message to paste it into a document. There is no separate PDF download in the chat.

## Take the kit to Olivia

When the kit is complete, click **Build My Story Bible With OliviaAI®**.

- **Builder or Studio:** a new Olivia conversation opens, seeded with your kit. Olivia starts reviewing it automatically.
- **$7 kit only:** a note appears under the button — *Upgrade to Builder to continue with OliviaAI® and build your Story Bible from this Starter Kit.* Clicking opens the Builder purchase modal.

If you already started Olivia from this kit, you get **Olivia Chat Already Exists**. Choose **Open Existing Olivia Chat** or **Start A New Olivia Chat** with a different title.

After you buy Builder from this screen, Stripe can return you to the same Simone thread and continue the handoff for you.

## If you cannot start a new idea

Without Builder/Studio and without a remaining $7 credit, **Talk to Simone** opens **Start Your Story with SimoneAI®**:

- **Upgrade to Builder** — unlimited Simone plus Olivia
- **Buy a Story Starter Kit for $7** — one new conversation

The app does not show a credit counter. If the $7 button does nothing useful, you already used that kit; buy another or upgrade.

## Pin, rename, delete

On **My Work**, each Simone card has a menu: **Pin**, **Rename**, **Delete**. Delete asks **Are you sure you want to delete this conversation? This action cannot be undone.**
