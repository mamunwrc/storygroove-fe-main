# 6. EllisAI®: developmental editing

Ellis is the developmental editor. She reads a manuscript you already wrote, delivers an editorial letter, then works chapter by chapter. She is **Studio** only. Builder accounts see **Edit with EllisAI®** and can upgrade from that modal.

Use Olivia to *write* a book from a Story Bible. Use Ellis to *revise* a finished or near-finished draft.

## Send a manuscript

1. On the dashboard, click **Start a New Edit**.
2. The modal **Send Your Manuscript to Ellis** asks for **Title of the book** and a file.
3. Accepted types: **Word (.doc, .docx), PDF, or TXT**. Maximum **50 MB**.
4. Follow the on-screen formatting checklist (clear chapter breaks, prologue/epilogue naming, series context). Ellis maps the book from those headings.
5. Click **Send to Ellis**.

You land in the upload studio (`/dashboard/upload/bookeditor/{id}`) with boot text: *Opening your manuscript…* / *Ellis is laying out your chapters and Manuscript Map.*

## The Ellis studio

| Panel | Name | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Left | **Manuscript Hub** | **Manuscript Map**, **Editorial Letter** |
| Center | Draft | Your imported chapters — revise here |
| Right | **Ellis' Editing Plan** | **Chapter Edits**, **Chapter Notes**, **Project Notes** |

Center coaching: *Revise your draft here while you work through Ellis' chapter-by-chapter developmental guidance. Tap Ellis' avatar anytime.*

### Editorial letter

Open **Editorial Letter**. First delivery walks two steps: **OK, got it.** then **Deliver My Editorial Letter**. Ellis can take a couple of minutes (*Ellis is reading your manuscript and writing your editorial letter…*).

Save with **Save Editorial Letter & Click on Ellis' Avatar Bottom Right To Continue**. Download from the hub: **Download Editorial Letter**. The map downloads as **Download Manuscript Map**.

Without Studio, the letter panel reads: *Ellis' editing is available on the Studio plan. Upgrade to get an editorial letter and scene-by-scene developmental edits.*

### Chapter-by-chapter review

Select a chapter. **Chapter Edits** shows **Chapter-by-Chapter Review**. Chat with Ellis from the avatar (title: **Ellis Developmental Editor and Manuscript Coach**).

When Ellis produces guidance you want to keep, click **Insert to Revision Plan**. That fills the editing plan Ellis uses for the rest of the book. **Download Editing Plan** exports it as `.docx`. If nothing has been inserted yet: *No chapter reviews have been inserted into your Revision Plan yet.*

**Download Manuscript** on the word-count bar exports your current draft as Word.

## If you are on Builder

**Edit with EllisAI®** explains: *Upgrade your Builder plan to Studio to unlock Ellis along with everything you already have in the novelist studio.* Choose Studio monthly or yearly. After a successful upgrade: *Upgraded to Studio! EllisAI® is now available.*

Trying to chat without Studio: *Your subscription does not include Ellis' editing.*

## What Ellis is not

Ellis is not line editing or copyediting as a separate product step. The upload studio is the live Ellis experience. A legacy **Final Draft** stepper still exists in the app for older finalize paths; new Ellis projects work in the upload studio above.
