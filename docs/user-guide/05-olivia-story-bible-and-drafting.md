# 5. OliviaAI®: Story Bible, outline, and drafting

Olivia is the plot strategist and book coach. She works in two rooms:

1. **Story Bible chat** — she interviews you and writes the Story Bible and character dossiers.
2. **Outline & Draft Studio** — the book editor, where she outlines fifteen scenes, coaches scene design, and sits beside you while you draft.

Olivia requires **Builder** or **Studio**. Simone-only accounts see **Build with OliviaAI®** and can buy Builder from that modal.

## Start a Story Bible

**From a finished Simone kit (recommended):** in Simone's chat, click **Build My Story Bible With OliviaAI®**. Olivia already has your title, genre, premise, protagonist, conflict, stakes, setting, subplots, and emotional payoff.

**From the dashboard:** click **Start a New Novel Plan**, enter **What's the name of your novel?**, then **Talk to Olivia**.

Click **Hey there writer! Click here to enter the OliviaAI® office.** A **Story Bible intake** strip tracks progress. Copy a finished Story Bible with the **Copy** button on the message that contains **📘 Story Bible**.

Intake is done when Olivia delivers **CHARACTER DOSSIERS**. The primary button becomes **Outline with OliviaAI®**.

## Open the Outline & Draft Studio

Click **Outline with OliviaAI®**. The app creates a novel from the Story Bible and opens `/dashboard/bookeditor/{id}`.

If an outline already exists for this Bible, you get **Outline Already Exists**: **Open Existing Outline** or **Create A New Outline** (needs a distinct working title).

Watch **Olivia's Guide** on first visit. The studio is split three ways:

| Panel | Name | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Left | **Story Hub** | **Outline**, **Characters**, **Story Bible** |
| Center | **Drafting Space** | Where you write the scene in your own words |
| Right | **Scene Coach** | **Scene Design**, **Scene Notes**, **Project Notes** |

The outline is built as **fifteen scenes** across acts. Drag the grip on a scene to reorder. When all fifteen have design, **Book Cover Concept** appears on the word-count bar.

## Work a scene

1. Select a scene in the outline.
2. Wait for or trigger **Scene Design** on the right. Olivia fills coaching for that scene. You can **Edit** / **Save** it.
3. Write in the **Drafting Space**. Tap Olivia's bubble (bottom right) any time.

**Open Olivia chat** is a floating coach. Stay in general chat, or work a specific scene (chip: **Coaching — {scene}**, then **Back to general chat**).

When Olivia produces a scene you want in the book:

- **✦ Insert into outline** — place it in the structure
- **✦ Save scene to outline...** — persist the generated scene

After the fifteen scenes exist, Olivia moves into **layering**: deepening scenes rather than adding the first pass. Keep inserting from chat; the studio tracks which scene is next.

Studio chat is for coaching, not dumping a whole chapter. If you paste more than about 1,000 words, Olivia tells you it looks like manuscript pages. Draft in the center panel; use Ellis if you already have a full manuscript.

**Focus Mode** hides chrome while you write. **Word Count** tracks progress toward the target.

## Download your work

On the word-count bar: **Download Manuscript** (`.docx`) once there is text. Disabled until then: *Add manuscript text to download*.

On the Outline tab: **Download Outline** (`.docx` of outline plus scene designs). It stays disabled until scene design exists: *Add scene design with Olivia first.* Success toast: *Outline ready — check your downloads folder.*

## If you do not have Builder

Clicks on Olivia open **Build with OliviaAI®**: *Choose the Builder plan to unlock Olivia and the full novel-building studio. SimoneAI® stays included with unlimited access.* Pick **Builder Monthly** or **Builder Yearly**, or **Compare all plans**.

Community in the sidebar appears once you have Olivia or Ellis access.
