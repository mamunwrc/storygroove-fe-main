# 3. Finding your way around

Logging in drops you on the dashboard, which greets you with **Welcome back, {your name}**. It has two views: a hub of coach cards for starting something new, and a project list for returning to something you already started.

## The coach hub

The default view is four cards.

**SimoneAI®** — **Start a New Idea.** Opens a small dialog asking for a project name, then takes you into a conversation with Simone. This is where a Story Starter Kit begins.

**OliviaAI®** — **Start a New Novel Plan.** Asks for your novel's name and opens a conversation with Olivia, who builds your Story Bible. You can also arrive here directly from a finished Simone kit, which carries your answers across.

**EllisAI®** — **Start a New Edit.** Opens the manuscript upload dialog. Studio plan only.

**My Work** — **Open My Work.** Switches to the project list.

Above the cards you may see banners about your subscription (paused, cancelled, or ending) and a **Your recent work** strip offering to drop you straight back into whatever you last had open. There is also a **Required First Step: Watch the Quick Start Tutorials** block with a **Watch Tutorials** button — worth doing before your first session with any coach, because each one expects you to drive the conversation in a particular way.

## My Work

The project list is headed **My Dashboard** and shows every piece of work you own, newest first, filtered by pills across the top:

- **All**
- **Simone Ideas** — Story Starter Kit conversations
- **Olivia Story Bible** — Story Bible conversations
- **Olivia Outline & Draft Studio** — novels you are outlining and drafting
- **Ellis' Edits** — uploaded manuscripts under developmental review

Each card carries the action that makes sense for its type — **Chat with SimoneAI®**, **Chat with OliviaAI®**, **Continue Writing**, or **View** — plus a menu offering **Pin**, **Rename** and **Delete**. Pinned projects float to the top. If a filter has nothing in it you get an empty state with a button to start one, for example **"No stories started with Simone yet."**

## The sidebar

The left sidebar is the constant navigation:

- **Dashboard** — back to the coach hub
- **Story Starter** — My Work filtered to Simone
- **Plot Strategist** — My Work filtered to Olivia
- **Developmental Editor** — My Work filtered to Ellis
- **Community** — the private writer community, shown if you have Olivia or Ellis access. While a subscription is paused the link stays visible but is disabled (community is on hold).
- **My Account** — profile and subscription

Superadmins additionally see **API Usage** and **Activity log**.

## What happens when your subscription lapses

If your subscription is paused or has been cancelled and the paid period has ended, the dashboard locks. You can still reach the dashboard itself and **My Account**, but opening a project redirects you to the Subscription tab with an explanation — *"Your subscription is paused. Resume billing to access this page."* or *"Your subscription has been cancelled. Please re-subscribe to access this page."* Your work is not deleted at that moment; it is locked until billing resumes. See [Managing your subscription](07-managing-your-subscription.md) for what pausing actually costs and preserves.
